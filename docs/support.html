Support

Tranquis  ·  Suftnet Limited

Need help with Tranquis? Email us at support@edquis.com and we'll reply within two working days.

Account

  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?. We'll email you a 6-digit code to set a new one.
  • Delete your account: open Settings in the app and tap Delete account. Your translation history, phrasebook and account details are deleted.

Tranquis Pro

  • Restore a purchase: open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel or change your plan: on iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tranquis. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.
  • Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Translations

  • Translations are produced by AI and can be wrong. Don't rely on them for medical, legal or safety decisions.
  • Voice and camera translation need microphone, camera or photo access, which you can change in iPhone Settings → Tranquis.
